Running a huddle
  • Open the session and press ⛶ Full screen (or the F key), then share your screen.
  • It advances by itself. Space pauses, ← and → step between scenes.
  • The blue line at the bottom of each scene is your script — read it, or say it your own way.
  • Pause on any scene where someone has a question; the timer stops with it.
  • Showing it on the office TV? The deck detects TV browsers and switches to large type with safe margins. If it still looks cramped, add ?tv=1 to the address; ?tv=0 forces normal mode.
  • Sound: narration is spoken by the browser, so it needs a laptop or desktop — most smart TVs have no speech engine. For a TV huddle, run it from a laptop and cast, or read the narration line aloud yourself.
